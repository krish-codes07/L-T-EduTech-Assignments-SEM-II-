# Task 02 - Complex Problem Table

## Course
Critical Thinking, Design Thinking, Leadership and Teamwork

## Topic
Design Thinking for Complex Problem Solving

## Objective
To identify complex problems from different application domains and justify why they are considered complex.

## Applications Chosen
1. Healthcare - Early Detection of Diseases Using AI
2. Smart Transportation - Traffic Congestion Management
3. Cybersecurity - Detection and Prevention of Advanced Cyber Attacks

## Outcome
The task demonstrates the identification and analysis of real-world complex problems across different domains using design thinking principles.
